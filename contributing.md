## ⚡ Why Phishing Awareness Matters

<div align="center">

| 🚨 **The Risk** | 💡 **What to Look For** | ✅ **Defensive Response** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Urgent requests designed to create panic | Unusual pressure or artificial deadlines | Pause and verify independently |
| Impersonation of trusted services | Lookalike domains and unfamiliar senders | Check the true destination and source |
| Requests for credentials or codes | Unexpected login, password, or MFA prompts | Never share secrets through unsolicited links |
| Suspicious attachments and downloads | Unexpected files or requests to enable macros | Use approved scanning and reporting channels |

</div>

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1️⃣ Review the software information

Use the button above to open the supplied software link. Check the publisher, package source, and any available documentation before downloading or running anything.

### 2️⃣ Confirm permissions and scope

Use security-testing tools only in your own environment or when you have explicit permission from the system owner. Define the purpose, participants, and limits of any exercise in advance.

### 3️⃣ Work in a controlled environment

Prefer a disposable lab, test accounts, and synthetic information. Do not collect real passwords, session tokens, or personal data as part of a training exercise.

### 4️⃣ Review lessons learned

Document warning signs, improve reporting procedures, and share defensive recommendations with the people involved.
